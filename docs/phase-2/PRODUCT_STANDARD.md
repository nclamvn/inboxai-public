# InboxAI 2.0 Product & Design Standard

Recorded 2026-11-11 from the owner's task and supplied prior discussion.
This reconstructs approved direction; original Library assets were not available.
Historical market claims, competitor prices and qualitative scores in that discussion
are contextual claims, not reverified research or acceptance evidence.

## User outcome
Open InboxAI to know what matters, who is waiting on you, who you are waiting on,
what changed, and what can be safely resolved. Keep a functional email client;
do not pivot the whole product into an ERP tool or generic chatbot.

| Pillar | Required behavior | Proof needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Action Inbox | Contextual ranking, reasons, evidence and correction learning | Important/urgent cases including low-authority senders, benign transactions and user corrections |
| Commitment Intelligence | Persistent ledger of owner, obligation, due time, changes, status and sources; update rather than duplicate | Thread evolution, moved deadlines, ambiguity, conflicts and completion with source links |
| Ask Inbox | Cross-account hybrid metadata/keyword/semantic retrieval, citations and abstention | Tenant isolation, contradictory/latest evidence, structured amounts/dates, unseen queries |
| AI Operator | Plan → Preview → Scope → Approve → Execute → Verify → Undo where feasible | Scope-bound approval, stale-plan rejection, idempotency, audit, verified outcomes and explicit undo limits |

Commitment Intelligence is the intended differentiation, Action Inbox the daily
entry point, Ask Inbox memory and AI Operator controlled execution. This is a
product hypothesis, not demonstrated customer value. Baseline email operations,
summaries and drafting support these pillars. No independent phishing engine,
fine-tuning or speculative CRM/calendar expansion in the initial refactor.

## Experience and visual authority
Latest decisions supersede the earlier Quiet Intelligence concepts:
Precision Neo-Flat 2027; navy #174A72, canvas #F7F9FA, surface #FFFFFF,
border #E1E7EB, text #192532; Inter; Lucide; 4px grid; straight 2px blue accents.
No purple, gradients, glassmorphism, glow, decorative AI glyphs, curved accents
or large color blocks that fragment the canvas. Refinement comes from hierarchy,
alignment, spacing, readable density, precise states and consistent interactions.

Today, Memory and Assistant are useful conceptual surfaces, not permission to
replace routing or rebuild every component now. Source emails remain accessible.
Cover desktop, tablet web/native and mobile web/native. Define keyboard/focus,
screen-reader, touch, loading, empty, error, offline and reduced-motion behavior.
Performance and accessibility are release criteria, not visual polish afterward.

## Value validation
Compare the narrow product workflow with a direct LLM and a tool-enabled agent.
Use the same held-out synthetic/consented data and declare model, tools, privacy,
human review, usable-output quality, cost and time. Synthetic fixtures prove
behavior, not real market advantage. Historical proposed 95% precision, 90% recall,
100% evidence and 0 unauthorized actions are proposed evaluation targets; they
have not been measured or adopted as blanket certification thresholds.

## Sequence
M0 security/baseline → M1 vault/sync/send/idempotency → M2 design/Action Inbox →
M3 ledger → M4 hybrid retrieval → M5 controlled operator → M6 platform/staging.
